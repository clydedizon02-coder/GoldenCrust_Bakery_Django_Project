About Golden Crust Bakery

Welcome to Golden Crust Bakery, a beloved neighborhood bakery nestled in the heart of Bacnotan, La Union. Since opening our doors, we have been dedicated to bringing the warmth of freshly baked goods to our community.

Our bakers wake up before dawn every day to prepare artisan breads, soft pandesal, flaky pastries, and sweet treats made with the finest local ingredients and traditional recipes passed down through generations.

We believe that good food brings people together. Whether you're a student from DMMMSU, a family looking for weekend treats, or a visitor exploring North La Union, there's always something warm and delicious waiting for you at Golden Crust.

Our Mission: To bake with love, serve with care, and make every customer feel at home.
